Boca del Rio,Veracruz. México

Veracruz has one of the oldest harbor in the country. It's boulevard has an extension of about 12km. Zócalo and Historic Center: The "heart of Boca" is home to the City Hall, with its arched façade, and the traditional Santa Ana Parish Church. Foro Boca: A modern concert and performing arts center featuring avant-garde architecture, located right by the sea. Agustín Lara House Museum: Also known as the "Casita Blanca" (Little White House), it is dedicated to the renowned Mexican musician and composer. Gastronomy: Renowned for fresh, Veracruz-style seafood, including the record-breaking feat of preparing the world's largest seafood-stuffed fish.
